01 — The transformation

From formal to playful.

The first iteration of Pawside's landing page was structurally sound but emotionally flat. It followed good design principles: clear hierarchy, proper spacing, readable copy. But it didn't feel like Pawside. It felt corporate. A design audit identified 7 structural problems. The redesign addressed all of them.

02 — Layout & Structure

Breaking the grid.

❌ Before
Rigid Grid
All sections stacked predictably. Two-column or one-column layouts. Even padding (60px everywhere). Maximum width constraint (960px). No surprise, no personality.
Hero: 1fr 1fr grid
Products: 3-column grid
Testimonials: 3-column grid
All centred, all aligned
✅ After
Asymmetrical
Hero grid 0.7fr 1.3fr (breathing space on left). Product cards staggered vertically (±40px offset). Ingredient cards rotated (-1° to +1°). Community cards rotated (±2°). Unpredictable, playful, energetic.
Hero: 0.7fr 1.3fr grid + 140px left padding
Products: Staggered +40px, 0px, -40px
Ingredients: Rotated ±1deg each
All full-width sections

From timid to bold.

❌ Before

BOLD full-width sections. Coral (#FF6B6B) ingredients section. Teal (#4ECDC4) products section. Yellow (#FFE66D) testimonials section. Colors own the space. Maximum vibrancy.

04 — Typography & Scale

From safe to playful.

❌ Before
Consistent Sizes
Hero h1: 64px. All section headings: 32-48px. No size variation. No hierarchy contrast. Elegant serif display, but reserved. DM Sans body at 15px. Safe, predictable.
Hero h1: 64px (elegant, reserved)
Section headings: 32-48px (consistent)
Body: 15px (safe)
No playful accents
✅ After
Dramatic Scale
Hero h1: 92px (MASSIVE). Callout text: 28px bold teal. Ingredient percentages: 32px serif (visual element, not just text). Bold sans-serif labels. Emoji badges in buttons. Extreme scale contrast = playful energy.
Hero h1: 92px (dominates)
Callout: 28px bold teal
Percentages: 32px serif (hero)
Emoji + bold accents

06 — Interactions & Animations

From static to playful.

❌ Before
Minimal Motion
Hover effects: subtle color change, slight lift (2-3px). No scroll animations. No page load animations. No micro-interactions. Page feels static and unresponsive. No delight.
Hover: color change
Hover: translateY(-3px)
No scroll animations
No page load effects
✅ After
Bouncy & Energetic
CTA button: scale(1.08) + rotate(-1deg) + bounce (cubic-bezier). Product cards: scale(1.08) + rotate(2deg). Paw prints: animated float (6s infinite). Hero content: slideInLeft on load. Testimonials: slideX on hover. Animations everywhere = energy.
CTA: cubic-bezier bounce
Products: scale + rotate
Paw prints: float animation
Load: slideIn animations
07 — The 7 Critical Changes

Design audit results.

Change 01: Grid Asymmetry
Hero grid changed from 1fr 1fr (equal) to 0.7fr 1.3fr (narrower content, wider image). Left padding increased from 60px to 140px. Result: breathing space on left, content feels centered.
Change 02: Full-Width Color Blocks
Removed max-width constraints. Sections now stretch edge-to-edge. Coral section, Teal section, Yellow section. Colors own the full viewport. Maximum impact.
Change 03: Oversized Typography
Hero h1 scaled from 64px to 92px. Added 28px bold teal callout. Ingredient percentages displayed in 32px serif (visual weight, not just text). Extreme scale = playful impact.
Change 04: Illustrated Elements
Added paw print watermarks (position:fixed, opacity:.08, animated float). Added emoji badges in buttons (🐾🛒). Added visual hierarchy through graphic elements, not just typography.
Change 05: Image Treatment
Hero image rotates -3deg. Cards rotate/stagger. Community cards rotate ±2deg. Images overlap sections. Images break the grid. Asymmetry creates energy.
Change 06: Micro-Interactions
CTA hover: scale(1.08) + rotate(-1deg) + cubic-bezier bounce. Product cards: scale(1.08) + rotate(2deg) on hover. Paw prints: float animation (6s infinite). Testimonials: slideX(20px) on hover. Animations throughout.
Change 07: Varied Spacing
Replaced uniform 60px padding with variable 40px-100px. Hero: 120px (tall, dominates). Ingredients: 120px (breathe). Products: 100px (balanced). Community: 120px (immersive). Spacing creates rhythm.

09 — Conclusion

From formal to alive.

The first iteration was correct in structure but wrong in spirit. A thorough design audit identified that the problem wasn't tactical (details) but strategic (layout, color, typography, spacing, animation). The redesign addressed all 7 structural issues. The result: a landing page that feels as playful and joyful as Pawside's copy. The design now matches the brand.
