The fire

Not every flame
cooks the same way.

Hickory burns slow and dense — it is for proteins that need time. Apple burns clean and sweet — it is for delicate fish that must not be overwhelmed. Cedar carries a resinous depth that belongs only to the mushroom course. Camphor smokes from a distance and never touches the food directly.
