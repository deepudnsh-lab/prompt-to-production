Full description for Behance

Verdana is a premium US clean beauty skincare brand built through a 14-step design process using Claude AI as the execution engine. THE CHALLENGE: The clean beauty market at the $80–$300 tier is dominated by two player types—heritage luxury (Tatcha) and disruptive science-first (Drunk Elephant). All competitors score high on either warmth or ingredient transparency, but none combine both with visual minimalism. THE RESEARCH: Structured brand research across 6 steps revealed the buyer (Sloane Mitchell, 29, UX Designer, Brooklyn) discovers on visual texture, verifies using chemical databases (INCIDecoder, CosDNA), and stops looking once a product proves itself. Competitive audit identified that no competitor in this tier combines high warmth with high ingredient visibility. THE SOLUTION: Direction C—Pared-back / Still. Ultra-minimal aesthetic paired with formula-first copywriting. Every design decision (from navbar detachment to ingredient placement as section 02) flows from research. WHAT'S INCLUDED: • Step 4: Industry research, market sizing, competitor scoring • Step 5: Buyer persona (Sloane Mitchell), purchase journey, design implications • Step 6: Competitor visual audit with annotated screenshots • Step 7: Three moodboard directions (Direction C selected) • Step 8: Voice rules and landing page structure • Step 9: Production landing page (all 11 images embedded, glassmorphism effects, 16px type, 20px image radius) • Step 10: Prompt diary with 10 verbatim prompts and 3 key lessons • Step 11: Nine design decisions vs category average • Step 12: Complete case study TOOLS: Claude AI (execution engine), Midjourney (moodboards and images), HTML/CSS (landing page), no-code design system. This is Brand 05 of 06 in "From Prompt to Production," a portfolio series demonstrating how structured research + AI execution produces distinctive brand identities.
