01 — Voice rules

Six rules for
every word.

Pawside's voice is playful and authentic, never apologetic or overly cutesy. The tone is confident, humorous, and irreverent — like talking to a dog-obsessed friend who actually knows their stuff. These six rules guide every word on every page.

Rule 01
Celebrate the dog, not the owner.
The dog is the star. The owner is the supporting cast. Never: "Treat yourself to premium grooming." Always: "Your dog deserves ingredients you can actually pronounce."
Rule 02
Humor over hype. Always.
Be funny first, sell second. A joke about dog breath is more memorable than a list of benefits. If the copy doesn't make us smile, it doesn't ship. Your dog sheds. We get it. Let's make that part easier.
Rule 03
Specific beats vague. Every time.
Not: "Premium oat blend." Yes: "5% Oat Extract from Minnesota farmers who talk to their crops." Specificity is funny. Vagueness is marketing. We are not doing marketing.
Rule 04
Own the awkwardness. Don't hide from it.
Dogs are weird. Dog ownership is weird. We celebrate that. Never pretend grooming is elegant or meditative — it's messy, it's chaotic, it's joyful. Lean into that reality.
Rule 05
Short sentences. Period. New thought.
No rambling. No fluff. One idea per sentence. If a sentence is longer than one line on mobile, cut it. Readers have the attention span of dogs (ironic, we know).
Rule 06
This is a standalone document. No references to other brands.
Define Pawside on Pawside's terms. Don't explain what we're not. Don't compare ourselves. We are exactly what the page says we are — nothing more, nothing less.
02 — Information architecture

Seven sections.
Seven questions.

The landing page structure follows Maya's purchase journey from Step 5. Each section answers one question she has, in the order she asks it. No fluff. No upsells. Just answers.

1
Hero Section
Is this brand for MY dog, or for Instagram dogs?
Headline: "Happy dog. Happy you." Subheading: "Grooming products that don't pretend to understand your dog better than you do." Visual: Dog mid-play, genuine joy, not posed. No filters. No fakeness.
2
What's Actually In It
What ingredients are we talking about, specifically?
Full ingredient breakdown with percentages. "5% Oat Extract. Soothes & hydrates." That's it. No flowery descriptions. No mystery blend. The formula does the talking.
3
Where It Comes From
Should I trust this? Where does this stuff actually come from?
Sourcing transparency map. "Oat Extract — Minnesota. Shea Butter — Ghana. Aloe Vera — Texas." Coordinates included. We are not hiding anything because we have nothing to hide.
4
The Product Lineup
What do you actually make? Do you have enough for a full routine?
Three core products. Shampoo. Conditioner. Detangling Spray. Each with honest descriptions. "This conditioner won't make your dog smell like a tropical resort. It will make their coat not suck."
5
Real Dogs. Real Results.
Has anyone like me actually used this? Did it work?
Three testimonials. Not from professional "brand ambassadors." From dog owners in dog groups. Before/after photos of actual dogs. Real captions. "My dog's coat went from dull to shiny. Also she sheds less now, which means less vacuuming, which means I have more time to scroll TikTok."
6
Join the Pack
Is there a community here? Or am I just buying shampoo?
Instagram community callout. "Tag us in your grooming wins. We repost the chaos." User-generated content takes over. The community is the product. The shampoo just makes it all possible.
7
Just Order It
What's the friction? Can I just buy this now?
One CTA. "Start the glow-up." Three reassurance signals: "Ships in 24 hours. 30-day glow-up guarantee. Join 10K+ happy dogs." No hidden costs. No fake scarcity. No pressure.
