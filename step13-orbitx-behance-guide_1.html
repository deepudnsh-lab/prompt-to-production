02 — Project description

The description is read by people who have already clicked in — they are interested. Answer three questions immediately: what is this, what was the process, what does it demonstrate. Under 250 words. Prose only — no bullet points.

Full project description — paste into Behance description field
~210 words · prose · no bullet points
OrbitX is the second brand in "From Prompt to Production" — a self-initiated portfolio series that documents a complete, research-led web design process executed through AI direction. Six hypothetical brands. Six industries. Fourteen steps each. Every design decision is made by the designer. Every output is produced through a prompt. The brief: design a civilian space tourism brand targeting ultra-high-net-worth passengers in Asia-Pacific. Differentiate from Blue Origin, Virgin Galactic, and SpaceX using research — not taste. The central insight, discovered through a competitor visual audit: the passenger is invisible on every competitor website. Despite Blue Origin having flown 58 civilians and Virgin Galactic having 700+ on a waitlist, not a single named passenger appears on any homepage. That gap became the design thesis: OrbitX builds the passenger. The process spans industry research (sourced market data, competitor pricing, design trends), customer persona development (Arjun Mehta, 52, founder, Mumbai/Dubai/Singapore), three moodboard directions generated on Midjourney, and a production-quality landing page built with a full-viewport AI video hero, three colour-graded section images, and a CSS Three.js starfield. The information architecture is structured as a mission briefing document — not a hospitality experience. Every section answers a specific question Arjun has at 11pm on a laptop in a hotel room. Tools used: Claude (Anthropic) · Midjourney · AI video generation · HTML / CSS / JS
