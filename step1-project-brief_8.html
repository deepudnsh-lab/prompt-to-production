01 — The big idea

A designer who uses
AI isn't being replaced.
They're being multiplied.

The design industry is in the middle of a seismic shift. Generative AI tools can produce landing pages, write copy, generate brand identities, and ship production-ready code — all in seconds. Many designers are responding with fear. This project responds with something different: a demonstration.

From Prompt to Production is a self-initiated Behance portfolio series that documents the complete process of designing six professional-grade landing pages across six completely different industries — entirely through conversation with Claude (Anthropic). No separate design tools. No manual file creation. No coding by hand. Every artifact in this project — the research, the personas, the moodboard directions, the copy, the layout concepts, the coded page, and the case study document itself — is produced through a directed AI workflow, guided by 13+ years of design leadership, brand strategy, and visual craft.

"Anyone can type a prompt. It takes a trained designer to ask the right questions, in the right order, with the right creative intent — and know when the output is wrong."
— The core thesis of this project

This project doesn't hide the AI. It celebrates it — and shows exactly what separates a designer who uses AI from someone who just uses AI.

02 — The problem being solved

What does a modern
design leader actually do?

After 13+ years leading design at companies like Interface.ai and Tact.ai — building brand identities, managing teams, shipping hundreds of assets quarterly — the question every employer is now asking is: can you work at the speed of AI?

The old expectation
Design is slow, expensive, and tool-dependent
Figma → wireframes → mockups → stakeholder reviews → developer handoff. Weeks per project. High resource cost.
The new reality
Design thinking × AI = a fully directed workflow
Brief → research → persona → moodboard direction → copy → layout concept → live HTML page. Every step executed through a single AI conversation. One creative director, zero separate tools.

This project is the proof of concept. Six live, production-quality landing pages — each with a fully documented case study showing every decision, every prompt, every iteration.

03 — The hypothesis
Design hypothesis
When a designer brings research, persona thinking, moodboarding, content strategy, and creative direction to AI prompting — the quality and speed of output raises a genuine question: what is the role of a design team in a world where one person can do this?

This will be tested across six distinct industries, each with a unique visual language, audience, and emotional register. The hypothesis either holds — or we learn exactly where AI still needs a human in the loop.

06 — The AI angle

What this project
does differently.

The differentiators
This project makes the process the product.
  • The prompt diary is fully documented. Every prompt sent, every output evaluated, every revision made — shown openly as part of the case study. The process itself is the deliverable, not just the final page.
  • Research comes before prompting. Industry data, competitor audits, and personas are completed before a single prompt is written — the same sequence a professional agency would follow.
  • The before/after comparison is honest. A basic AI output with a generic prompt is shown alongside the strategically directed result — so the viewer can judge the difference for themselves.
  • The creative director is always in control. AI handles production; the designer handles vision, judgment, taste, and strategy. Every decision in the prompt diary is explained and owned.
  • Six industries, zero repetition. Each website has a completely different visual language, tone-of-voice, and design approach — demonstrating range across categories, not depth in one.
